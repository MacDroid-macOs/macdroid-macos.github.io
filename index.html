How It Works

1. Connect

Plug your Android phone or tablet into your Mac with a USB cable. Enable USB debugging if prompted—takes seconds.

2. Mount in Finder

MacDroid mounts your device as a drive. All storage appears instantly in Finder sidebar.

3. Transfer or Edit

Drag files, folders, or media between Mac and Android. Edit directly—no extraction needed.

4. Eject Safely

Right-click to eject. Unplug. Your files are synced and ready.
